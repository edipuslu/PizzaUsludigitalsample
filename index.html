Our Story

Born in a Turkish
Kitchen, Loved Everywhere

Uslu Pizza started in 2012 when chef Mehmet Uslu brought his grandmother's dough recipe from Izmir to the city. Combined with the finest Italian techniques and locally sourced ingredients, each pizza tells a story of two cultures united by fire.

Our stone oven reaches 900°F — the same temperature Neapolitan masters have used for centuries. That's how we guarantee that leopard-spotted crust and perfectly melted cheese every single time.
